# What a typical hackathon involves

Domain background for `hackathon-judge`. Reason from this baseline only where
the event's rules are silent, and say when the baseline supplied the value.
Nothing here overrides a stated rule.

- **Duration:** 24–48 hours of wall clock, of which 12–20 are productive
  build time after travel, team formation, meals, and sleep.
- **Team:** 1–4 people. Assume the stated team only; no reinforcements.
- **Judging format:** a 2–5 minute live demo plus Q&A, or a science-fair
  table round where judges spend 2–3 minutes per project. Judges see 20–60
  projects and remember a handful.
- **"Done" on stage:** a working path through one scenario, run live or from
  a recorded fallback, that a non-expert recognises as valuable within 30
  seconds.

## Standard failure modes

- **Over-scoping** — building the whole product instead of one path.
- **Integration hell** — an unfamiliar API, auth, or deploy step eating a
  third of the clock.
- **Demo-day flakiness** — venue wifi, rate limits, cold starts, a live model
  call.
- **Pre-work disqualification** — committing code before the start, or
  reusing an existing project beyond what the rules allow.
- **Submission misses** — no video, late repo, missing sponsor tech.
